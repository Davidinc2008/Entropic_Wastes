Entropic Wastes

It has been eons since Kerbals initially began exploring the stars. Kerbalkind has set foot on many different bodies. Unfortunately, the eons haven't been nice to Kerbalkind, as every so often, stars would die and force Kerbals to move elsewhere. Thankfully, the Kerbals have gotten used to moving to other star systems. They've moved to the Tempus System (Beyond Home), the Harmonia System (Celestial Harmony), and many, many others. However, it appears that time is running out for the Kerbals. The expansion of the universe has prevented the creation of stars to replace the dying ones. Kerbals now live on Hope, a tropical planet which may end up being their final homeworld. Hope orbits Bastion, an aging red-dwarf that has become a blue-dwarf! Explore the Bastion system and uncover its secrets, and explore other exotic stars which all have seen better days as you inch closer to Finality, the center of the galaxy. Finality may hold the secret to helping Kerbals live in the entropic age.
